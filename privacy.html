Privacy Policy

Worthii Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 24, 2026 · Applies to Worthii (com.moyu.worthit) and Worthii China Edition (com.moyu.worthit.cn)

1. Privacy by Design

Worthii is built around local-first mindful spending. We do not use third-party advertising SDKs, data brokers, or cross-app tracking (NSPrivacyTracking = false).

2. Data Stored Locally & in Your Private iCloud

  • Spending & Wish Records: Salary calibration, work hours, selected currency, expenses, income, cooldown wishes, and behavioral insight summaries are stored locally on your device and optionally backed up to your private Apple iCloud (CloudKit Private Database).
  • Home Screen Widget Snapshot: Only a compact, non-sensitive summary (saved amount, saved workdays, active cooldown item name & timer, active companion) is shared with the Worthii Home Screen Widget via the device-local App Group container.
  • Local Behavioral Coach: Peak impulse time bands and resist rates are calculated strictly on-device and never transmitted to external analytics servers.

3. Edition Differences (Global vs. Mainland China)

  • Global Edition (com.moyu.worthit): Includes optional social features (Worthii Circle, Friends, 1-to-1 Chat, Friendship Promises). Only content you explicitly choose to share or send is stored in Apple CloudKit to deliver those features. Reporting a post or blocking a user hides content immediately and records a moderation report for review.
  • Mainland China Edition (com.moyu.worthit.cn): Operates as a standalone personal cooldown journal (with personal iCloud backup) and does not upload posts to a public social feed.

4. Photos & Optional Media

Photo Library access is requested only when you tap to attach a photo to a wish journal entry or optional payment instruction. Images remain on-device unless you explicitly publish a wish photo in the Global edition's optional Circle.

5. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

All payments (auto-renewing Pro subscriptions, one-time Lifetime Pro unlock, and optional Time Coin packs) are processed exclusively by Apple via StoreKit 2. Worthii never receives or stores your payment card details. Signed StoreKit transaction evidence is verified on-device to unlock your entitlements.

6. Account Deletion & Data Retention

You can delete your Worthii account at any time inside the app (Profile → Settings → Delete Account). Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, backup records, and social data, leaving only a minimal technical deletion tombstone so older devices do not resurrect deleted data. Auto-renewing Apple subscriptions must still be canceled in iOS Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.

7. Contact

For privacy inquiries or data assistance, please visit our Support Page.

值得么（Worthii）隐私政策

1. 核心隐私原则

值得么坚持本地优先的理性消费理念。我们不接入任何第三方广告 SDK，不出售数据，不进行跨 App 行为追踪（NSPrivacyTracking = false）。

2. 本地与个人 iCloud 数据

  • 账本与心愿冷静期记录： 您的月薪、工时、币种、记账流水、心愿冷静期与消费模式洞察均优先保存在设备本地，并通过您个人的 Apple iCloud（CloudKit 私有数据库）进行安全备份。
  • 桌面小组件快照： 为展示累计省下工时与冷静期倒计时，应用仅通过本机 App Group 共享脱敏摘要数据，不上传第三方服务器。
  • 冲动洞察教练： 冷静成功率、高发冲动时段和最常忍住分类完全在设备本地计算，绝不离机上传。

3. 大陆版与国际版差异

  • 中国大陆版（com.moyu.worthit.cn）： 为纯净的个人冷静记账与感悟手账版本（含个人 iCloud 同步），不含公开社区动态上传。
  • 国际版（com.moyu.worthit）： 提供可选的 Worthii 圈子、好友与一对一消息。仅当您主动发布或发送时，相关内容才会存入 CloudKit 以提供对应功能。

4. 相册权限说明

仅当您主动为心愿手账添加配图或设置可选图片时才会请求相册访问权限，应用不会在后台读取您的相册。

5. 应用内购买与订阅

所有付费项目（自动续期 Pro 会员、一次性买断永久 Pro 及可选光阴币包）均由 Apple 通过 StoreKit 2 统一结算。我们不收集也不保存您的银行卡或支付账号信息，仅在本地校验 Apple 签名的交易凭证以发放权益。

6. 账号注销与数据删除

您可随时在应用内「我的 → 设置 → 注销账号」永久删除您的个人资料与云端备份。系统仅保留极简的防复活删除标记（Tombstone），防止旧设备重新覆盖已删除数据。若您开通了自动续期订阅，请同时在 iOS「设置 → Apple 账户 → 订阅」中取消续费。

7. 联系与支持

如需隐私咨询或账号协助，请访问 使用支持页面。
