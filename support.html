Quick answers for Worthii (Global) & 值得么 (China Edition)

How are work hours calculated?

Worthii divides your monthly income by your monthly workdays and daily work hours to calculate your hourly rate, then converts any item price into exact work hours and workdays. You can update salary, schedule, or currency anytime in Profile → Settings. Changing currency does not retroactively convert historical amounts.

Pro Subscription vs. Lifetime Pro

Worthii Pro is available as Monthly, Yearly, or one-time Lifetime Pro. All plans unlock 30 active cooldown slots, 72-hour piggy-bank reward storage, 2× daily coin rewards, CSV record export, and the exclusive diamond frame & Pro badge. If you switch from an active subscription to Lifetime Pro, remember to cancel the auto-renewing subscription in iOS Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.

Restore Purchases or iCloud Sync

Ensure you are signed into the same Apple Account (iCloud & App Store) on your device, then open Profile → Worthii Pro → Restore Purchases or tap Sync Now in Profile.

Delete Account

Go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account. Account deletion erases your Worthii profile and cloud backup permanently. Remember to manage any active Apple subscription separately in iOS Settings.

Need Direct Assistance?

Submit a support request or bug report on our official support tracker: Worthii Support Desk.

常见问题与协助

适用于 值得么（大陆版）与 Worthii（国际版）

工时是如何换算的？

应用根据您设置的月收入、每月工作日和每日工时自动算出时薪，并将商品价格实时换算为需要工作的小时数与天数。您可随时在 我的 → 设置 修改薪资、工时或货币单位；切换货币不会自动换算旧账历史金额。

连续订阅与永久买断版（Lifetime Pro）

Pro 会员提供按月、按年订阅及一次性付费的 永久买断版，均包含 30 个同时冷静舱、72 小时存钱罐收益上限、每日 2 倍光阴奖励、账单导出与专属钻石边框标识。若您此前开通了自动续期订阅并改购永久版，请在 iOS 设置 → Apple 账户 → 订阅 中取消原自动续期。

恢复购买与 iCloud 同步

请确认设备已登录购买时使用的同一 Apple 账户并开启 iCloud，随后在 我的 → 升级 Pro → 恢复购买 即可自动核验并恢复权益。
